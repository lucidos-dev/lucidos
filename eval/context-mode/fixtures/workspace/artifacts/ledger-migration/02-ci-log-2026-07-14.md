## Agreed at triage

Recorded at the 08:30Z triage call, before the postmortem was written. The
postmortem supersedes this list and adds owners.

1. Do not run dry run 5 until the lease defect is fixed. A green run against
   the current code would not mean anything.
2. Make `seq-check` unconditional. Delete the `RUN_SEQ_CHECK` gate rather
   than setting the variable.
3. Rebuild the unique index inside the backfill step, not after it. The index
   is the mechanism that would have made this loud.
4. Renew the lease at a fraction of its lifetime, not at its full lifetime.
5. Compare planned rows against written rows at the end of the backfill, and
   fail on a mismatch.

Item 5 is the cheapest of the five. The two numbers were already printed on
adjacent lines, and nothing compared them.

## Reading this log later

The failure is visible in three places and easy to miss in all three. The
lease warning at 02:11:47 is a `WARN` in a step that passed. The row count
mismatch at 03:38:18 is two numbers on one line with no comparison. The
skipped step at 03:44:07 reads like normal pipeline output.

Only `parity-check` said anything was wrong, and it ran last. That is the
shape of the problem, and it is why the cutover runbook now gates on checks
rather than on a pipeline result.
